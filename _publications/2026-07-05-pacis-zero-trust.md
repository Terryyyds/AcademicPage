**Yu Deng and Anushia Inthiran** (2026). *PACIS 2026 Proceedings*, 10. Complete paper, **PACIS2026-1766**.

This pilot study explores the factors associated with perceived need for Zero Trust in SMEs and the practical barriers to adoption. It outlines a staged approach to implementation in organizations with limited resources.
