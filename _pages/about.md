I am Yu Deng. My research interests include Zero Trust Architecture and information systems security readiness in small and medium enterprises (SMEs).

I co-authored a paper published in the *PACIS 2026 Proceedings* and presented this work at PACIS 2026 in Jakarta, Indonesia.

## Featured publication

**Towards Zero Trust Architecture: A Pilot Study on Information Systems Security Readiness amongst Small and Medium Enterprises**

Yu Deng and Anushia Inthiran (2026). *PACIS 2026 Proceedings*, 10.

The pilot study examines how SMEs perceive the need for Zero Trust and the barriers to adoption.

[Official proceedings record](https://aisel.aisnet.org/pacis2026/dig_sec/dig_sec/10/) · [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18901)
