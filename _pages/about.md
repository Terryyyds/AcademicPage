I am **Yu (Terry) Deng (邓煜)**, a PhD student in **Management Science and Engineering at Xi'an Jiaotong University**, where I focus on machine learning. I hold a master's degree in Business Information Systems from the University of Canterbury and a bachelor's degree in Network and New Media from Shanghai Sanda University.

My research interests connect machine learning, information systems and cybersecurity. I am particularly interested in AI and large language model security, Zero Trust Architecture, and the organizational and behavioral factors that shape technology adoption.

## Research

My work on SME security readiness was published and presented at **PACIS 2026** in Jakarta. I also co-authored **MILE**, a preprint on an exoskeleton and visuotactile robotic-hand system for collecting dexterous-manipulation data.

- **Zero Trust and SME readiness:** [Official PACIS proceedings](https://aisel.aisnet.org/pacis2026/dig_sec/dig_sec/10/) · [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18901)
- **MILE:** [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.00324)
